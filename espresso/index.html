How these numbers are calculated

The coffee calendar, monthly records and streak use a 2:30 AM local day boundary. Before 2:30 AM belongs to the previous coffee day and may count toward the previous month. Earliest/latest rank the time within that coffee day, not the chronological first/last date of the month. Records and streaks include all archived attempts. Ties are displayed. A streak through yesterday stays active while today is still in progress.

Total espresso output sums the last recorded extraction-phase weight for eligible shots only, excluding missing or negative endpoints; it is not measured consumption. This differs from the peak brew/drain weight used in the existing per-shot metrics. Existing daily averages and weekday bars still use midnight-based dates.

All timestamps use the displayed local timezone. A saved shot is a cup proxy and can include an aborted brew; this is an archive count, not a physical cup counter. Heating and purge are excluded by matching recorded states to the profile’s stage names.

Brew time includes pre-infusion. Main extraction time excludes stages identified as pre-infusion. Aborted shots identified by explicit markers, shots at or above the duration limit, and implausible weights are excluded from the daily numbers but kept in the archive. The eligibility counts and exclusion reasons are shown above. Within a shot, measured pressure and temperature are weighted by the time represented by each sample; across eligible shots, each available shot mean has equal weight. Missing measurements are excluded, never treated as zero. The sample count appears beneath each average.

Output weight is the peak nonnegative scale reading during brewing and immediate draining, before cup removal/purge. This estimate can include scale noise. Target temperature comes from the profile and is separate from measured temperature. Pre-infusion detection uses configurable stage names/patterns; unnamed or differently named phases may need local configuration.

Archive counts, calendar/weekday plots and profile totals include all archived attempts. The daily numbers and per-date measurement averages use eligible shots only. Daily averages span the first archived shot through the statistics date, including quiet days. Weekday averages use the number of Mondays, Tuesdays, etc. in that same period. Only history still available when collection began can be imported.
